Note: btn-small is the default and can be omitted, unless needed for a variant (e.g. lg:btn-small).

Example: Small button (default) changing to medium button at medium breakpoint and large button at large breakpoint

<button type="button" class="tw-btn-primary md:tw-btn-medium lg:tw-btn-large">Button</button>

Note: btn-large is currently only available for the Primary button

Example in reverse: Medium or large button changing to a small button at a larger breakpoint

<button type="button" class="tw-btn-primary tw-btn-large md:tw-btn-medium lg:tw-btn-small">Button</button>

Note: [aria-disabled="true"] should be preferred over the disabled attribute to support screen readers. disabled makes a button completely invisible to screen readers, so use [aria-disabled="true"] instead and prevent the action with javascript. The [aria-disabled="true"] styles are updated with higher contrast outlines to meet WCAG guidelines and the disabled are not currently.
